Getting Started

Basic Workflow

  1. Enter tree measurements (circumference and height)
  2. Configure pricing in Settings
  3. Click "Full Estimate" to add customer details and generate a quote
  4. Export as PDF to share with customers

Features

Measurements

  • Circumference - measured at breast height (approximately 4.5 feet)
  • Height - total height of the tree
  • Calculates volume in cubic feet or cubic meters

Estimates

  • Starting fee (base charge)
  • Price per unit volume
  • Optional tax calculations
  • Optional discounts
  • Customer information fields
  • Job notes section

Multiple Trees

Add multiple trees to a single estimate. All volumes combine for total cost calculation.

Photos

Attach photos to estimates. Optional to include in PDF exports.

History

Access previous estimates and calculations saved on your device.

Company Branding

Common Questions

How accurate are the calculations?

Tree volume is calculated using the formula: (circumference × circumference × height) / 12.56. Results are estimates based on input measurements. You are responsible for verifying accuracy before providing estimates to customers.

How do I measure circumference?

Use a measuring tape around the trunk at breast height (about 4.5 feet from the ground).

Can I edit estimates after saving?

Access saved estimates from History. You can modify customer information, notes, discounts, and other details.

How do I share an estimate?

In Full Estimate view, tap "Share / Export PDF" to generate a PDF and share via email or other apps.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to external servers.

Can I delete estimates?

Yes, from the History tab you can swipe to delete or access options to remove estimates.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, Tree Cost Calculator works entirely offline. No internet connection is required.

What if the PDF doesn't look right?

Try adjusting your photo settings or PDF viewer. Some images may need resizing before exporting.

Troubleshooting

App crashes when opening History

You may have too many saved estimates. Try deleting older ones and restarting the app.

Photos aren't showing in PDF

Make sure "Include Photo in PDF" is enabled in Settings and photos were successfully added to the estimate.

Calculations seem wrong

Verify your settings (pricing, tax rate) are correct. Double-check tree measurements. All calculations are estimates—verify before use.

Can't find an old estimate

Check History. Estimates are stored on this device only. If you uninstalled the app or restored from backup, previous data may not be available.
