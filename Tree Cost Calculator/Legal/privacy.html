Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

Tree Cost Calculator is a mobile application that helps tree service professionals calculate and manage tree removal estimates. This privacy policy describes our data practices and your privacy rights.

Data Storage

Tree Cost Calculator stores all data locally on your device. This includes:

  • Tree measurements and calculations
  • Customer names and locations
  • Pricing and discount information
  • Estimate history
  • Photos attached to estimates
  • Job notes and observations
  • Company branding information

We do not transmit, collect, or store this information on external servers.

What We Don't Collect

Tree Cost Calculator does not collect, track, or transmit:

  • Personal information about you or your customers
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • IP addresses
  • Browsing or app activity logs

Photos

Photos you attach to estimates are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to us or external servers unless you explicitly share them through email, messaging, or other sharing methods.

Exporting and Sharing

When you export an estimate as a PDF or share it through your device, that action is performed entirely on your device. We have no access to PDFs or information you share. You are responsible for managing any information included in shared documents.

Data Security

Your data security depends on your device's security. We recommend:

  • Keeping iOS updated with the latest security patches
  • Using device-level security (Face ID, passcode)
  • Protecting physical access to your device
  • Managing device backups appropriately

Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  • Delete individual estimates from the app's history
  • Uninstall the application to remove all local data
  • Remove data from device backups through your backup service settings

No Guarantees

Tree Cost Calculator is provided as-is. We do not guarantee that:

  • Calculations will be accurate for all situations
  • The app will be available at all times
  • The app will function without errors
  • Exported PDFs will format correctly in all applications

You are responsible for verifying all calculations and estimates before providing them to customers.

Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of the current policy.
